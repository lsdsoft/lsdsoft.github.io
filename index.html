Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

LSD Software ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Call Screening Agent Android application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

1. Core Privacy Commitment: 100% Local Processing

Call Screening Agent is designed with privacy as its primary foundation. All screening operations, phone number comparisons, and call logs remain strictly on your physical Android device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal data, phone numbers, contacts, or call logs to external servers or third parties under any circumstances.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The application processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Incoming Phone Numbers & Call Logs: Phone numbers of incoming callers are processed in real-time by the application's screening service to determine whether to allow or block the call. Blocked call history (including phone number and timestamp) is stored in a local, encrypted Room database on your device.
  • Whitelist Data: Numbers added to your Whitelist (either manually or via the Android Process Text context menu) are stored locally in your device's private App Preferences (`SharedPreferences`). Whitelisted numbers automatically expire after 7 days and are cleaned locally.
  • Contact List Access: If granted, the application queries your device's address book (`ContactsContract`) strictly to identify whether incoming callers are known contacts. Your contacts are never read out of your device or transmitted off-device.

3. Use of Permissions

To perform its core functions as a call screening utility, the app requires specific Android permissions:

  • Call Screening Role (`ROLE_CALL_SCREENING`): Required to intercept incoming phone calls and automatically block unwanted numbers.
  • Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Required to display user-configurable daily and weekly summaries of blocked call activity, as well as active screening status alerts.
  • Process Text (`ACTION_PROCESS_TEXT`): Allows users to highlight phone numbers in other applications and add them directly to the local app whitelist.

4. Data Retention & User Control

You have total control over all data generated by Call Screening Agent:

  • Call history entries can be individually or completely cleared directly within the application's main log screen.
  • Uninstalling the application immediately removes all locally saved databases, whitelists, and preferences permanently from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect or request personal information from anyone under the age of 13. All data remains local to the device owner.
